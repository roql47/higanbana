# Ogimachi survey data

Elevation and photographic base: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (国土地理院), processed by this project.
https://maps.gsi.go.jp/development/ichiran.html
https://www.gsi.go.jp/kikakuchousei/kikakuchousei40182.html

Building/road/agricultural geometry: © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License (ODbL).
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The OSM-derived geometry is available in survey.json. Authored model choices, photograph-traced foreground plots, woodland masks and terrain grading are project modifications, not official mapping or measured conservation models.

Full raw source manifest, acquisition query and modeling limitations are retained in the repository under assets/reference/ogimachi-gis and docs/ogimachi-survey-north-2026-09-10.md.
