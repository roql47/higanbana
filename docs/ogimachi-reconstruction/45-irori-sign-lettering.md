# Centered sign and authored lettering

The sign bounding-box center is aligned with the entrance local Y=0, a -0.419943 m shift from v5. The crest follows the slab. Font objects were replaced by three editable mesh glyphs made from manually authored Catmull-Rom stroke centerlines with variable width, tapered endings and subtle edge irregularity. Existing worn ivory paint material is retained. This is an authored interpretation, not a tracing of the restaurant logo.
